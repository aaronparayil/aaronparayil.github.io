About

Desktop app · Windows  /  Nov 2026 – now

          
RaptorSense


          

            A free, open-source replacement for Acer's PredatorSense and NitroSense apps on Predator and Nitro laptops. It drives the keyboard lighting and controls fans and power, with no account and no telemetry. Tested on a Predator Helios 16 and a Nitro 5.
          


          
    
            
  • Up to 147 lighting effects, including ones that follow your music and your typing
    • 
            
  • A frame-by-frame editor for your own keyboard animations, shareable as files
    • 
            
  • Fan speeds, power profiles, temperatures and automations like "Turbo while this game is open"

02 / 03

          
Browser game · 3D  /  Aug 2025 – now

          
Rig Builder


          

            A 3D PC-building game that runs in the browser. You walk around a showroom of real parts, take them to the workbench and build a PC step by step, then power it on and benchmark it. The whole game is under 1 MB.
          


          
    
            
  • Real compatibility rules: CPU sockets, DDR4 vs DDR5, cooler brackets, power budgets
    • 
            
  • A POST check on power-on, then frame-rate estimates at 1080p, 1440p and 4K
    • 
            
  • Six budget challenges, side-by-side part comparisons, and full VR on WebXR headsets
    • 
          


          

            three.js

Web app · ML  /  Aug–Sep 2026

          
HavaMana
